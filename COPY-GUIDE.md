# Website voice and visitor intent

Use these principles for future site edits, based on Jeremy’s feedback:

- Write to the visitor, in Jeremy’s voice. Do not explain the design or strategy to Jeremy inside site copy.
- Make it clear what Jeremy does, what the visitor will find, and why it might interest or help them.
- Use concrete section headings and destinations: articles, projects being built in public, professional background, photography, conversations.
- Name the next step in links: View my projects, Explore the articles, Meet Jeremy.
- Explain benefits with specific uses rather than broad claims about curiosity, connection, or transformation.
- Present developing apps honestly as prototypes or works in progress; do not imply a live build stream or unverified outcomes.
- Keep the signature Stay curious / Stay connected headline and visual identity.
- Let technical experience lead; Motion Visual Media supports Jeremy’s creative story farther down the page.
- Apply this approach to homepage, archive, follow prompts, and contact copy. Preserve published article content and dates unless an editorial update is requested.
